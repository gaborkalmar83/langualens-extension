    Where your text goes

    Translation runs on your own machine using Chrome's built-in translator. The text you select and the pages you read are never sent to a translation server, and there are no accounts, analytics or advertising.

    Two things use the network: Chrome downloading a language model once per language pair, which transfers the model and not your text; and speaking text aloud, which hands it to the speech voice you have installed, and some voices are cloud based.

    Saved words stay in this browser's local extension storage until you export or clear them.
